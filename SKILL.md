## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design rebuilds the subject and its necessary relations as one iced-cookie bas-relief miniature: rounded contours, matte frosting, fondant and soft-clay, small-stamp scale and abundant whitespace. It is not a photographic cookie filter, a plastic 3D render, a filled scene, a cheap cartoon or a template icon. Translate source colours into bright, soft icing rather than copying muddy tones.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-226/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
